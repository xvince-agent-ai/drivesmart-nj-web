FAQ

Questions people ask before they download.

Is this an official NJ MVC app?

No. DriveSmart NJ is an unofficial study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission. Always confirm rules in the official NJ Driver Manual.

Do I need an account?

No. Guests can study and use a free set of AI tokens. Sign in if you want Lifetime Access and credits to follow you across devices.

What score do I need to pass?

The NJ knowledge test is 50 questions. You need at least 40 correct (80%).

Can I study offline?

Yes. Study materials and practice questions are available offline once downloaded.

What is Test Readiness?

An app-generated index from your practice and mock results. It is not a calibrated probability of passing the real exam.
